Privacy Policy

Draft for review. Written to match India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Have it checked by a lawyer before public launch.

Data fiduciary: Tarak B Patel, an individual in India. Grievances: [add a contact email before launch].

1. What we collect

  • Usertag: the public name you choose.
  • Email address: used once to send your sign-in code. We store only a keyed hash of it, not the address itself.
  • Device token: a random secret in a browser cookie. We store only its hash.
  • Game data: matches played, wins, kills, experience points, and friends.
  • Server logs: request times and addresses, kept for security and then deleted.

2. Why we use it

To run your account, verify it is you, let you play and add friends, keep your progress, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Cookies

We use one essential cookie that keeps you signed in on this browser. It is HttpOnly and has no tracking purpose. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

4. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account at any time, and we will delete your usertag, email hash, and game data within a reasonable time.

5. Your rights under the DPDP Act

You may ask for access to your data, correction of it, its erasure, and a grievance redressal. Write to the contact address above. We will respond within the time the law requires.

6. Sharing

We share data only with the email provider that delivers sign-in codes, and only as needed to send them. Other players see your usertag and game statistics.

7. Security

We hash tokens and email addresses, store them with restricted file permissions, and serve the game over HTTPS in production. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your mailbox safe.

8. Children

The service is for players aged 18 and over. Under the DPDP Act a child is anyone under 18, and processing a child's data needs verifiable parental consent, which the service does not collect. If we learn that a child has an account, we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the latest version is shown below.
